### 🗺️ Ecosystem & Architectural Synergy Clusters

My open-source and personal engineering portfolio is organized into 5 coherent, synergistic domains:

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h4>🌐 1. GIS, Geodesy & Surveying Platform</h4>
      <p>High-precision computational geometry and geospatial data pipelines.</p>
      <ul>
        <li><b><a href="https://github.com/drsntsn/cokgen_alan_hesabi">cokgen_alan_hesabi</a></b>: Python Shoelace Gauss formula for arbitrary polygons, perimeter, centroid, and bounding box analysis.</li>
        <li><b>geodesy_master</b>: Ellipsoidal calculations, datum shifts, and geodetic reference models.</li>
        <li><b>cevir-turkiye-d</b>: Turkish projection and local coordinate transformation utilities.</li>
        <li><b>sondaj</b>: Geotechnical borehole logging, stratigraphic profile generation, and automated engineering reports.</li>
      </ul>
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h4>🧪 2. Industrial Density & Chemistry Tools</h4>
      <p>Laboratory-grade physical chemistry calculators and physical calibration engines.</p>
      <ul>
        <li><b><a href="https://github.com/drsntsn/bomecetveli">bomecetveli</a></b>: Precision Baumé degree (°Bé) and Sodium Sulfate (Na₂SO₄) density converter powered by empirical monotonic calibration tables.</li>
        <li><b>Cozelti</b>: Chemical solution concentration, mixing proportions, and dilution calculation algorithms.</li>
      </ul>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h4>⚡ 3. High-Precision Algorithms & Core Utilities</h4>
      <p>Mathematical engines free of floating-point drift.</p>

        <li><b><a href="https://github.com/drsntsn/toplama_js">toplama_js</a></b>: IEEE 754 float-safe number accumulator, real-time statistical aggregator (Mean, Median, Range, StdDev), interactive scaling multiplier, and undo/redo history.</li>
        <li><b>css_only_floating_label</b>: Zero-JS accessible modern floating label form design system.</li>
      </ul>
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

        <li><b>qr_menu_web</b>: Administrative management dashboard for menu items, pricing, categories, and analytics.</li>
        <li><b>qr_menu_flutter</b>: Cross-platform iOS/Android mobile client with real-time dining table ordering.</li>
      </ul>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
